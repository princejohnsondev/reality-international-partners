Godfried is a business leader and a risk professional with 19 years of relevant experience, 10 years of which is board-room level experience in the banking and finance industry. He has demonstrated expertise in capital raising, financial and banking regulatory advisory, financial and cyber risk management, credit management, capital and debt recovery, business development, and corporate restructuring.

He has held senior positions including Risk, Compliance and Finance Executive of Kuda Ghana Limited; Chief Risk Officer of Bayport Savings and Loans Limited; Managing Director of ABii National Savings and Loans; Head of Credit of Bank of Africa; Head of Risk Management at Bank of Africa; Head of Recovery of Bank of Africa; and Senior Credit and Risk Analyst of Société Générale Ghana. He was the first managing director of ABii National Savings and Loans and led the institution from 2013 to 2017.

He holds a BSc in Civil Engineering from the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology, Ghana, and an MSc in Banking and Finance from the University of Lausanne, Switzerland. He is a member of the Global Association of Risk Professionals.

Dr. Kusi is an investment banker. He has over twenty years of experience in investment banking, strategic management, treasury management, financial analysis, fund management, and corporate valuations. He worked as the Chief Executive of uniSecurities GH Limited until 2016, having had prior experience as General Manager at uniBank GH Ltd and as Head of Operations with SIC Financial Services Limited. He also worked as a General Manager at SDC Capital Ltd after working with NTHC Limited as Head of Corporate Finance and Research. His experience includes a seven-year period with Social Security and National Insurance Trust (SSNIT) as a corporate planning officer and later as Deputy Head of Treasury.

He served as a board member of the Bank of Ghana and was committee chairman for auditing and a committee member of strategic planning and budget until 2017. He also served on the board of UMB Bank and serves on the board of Millennium Insurance Limited.

He holds a PhD in Finance (Financial Economics) from the University for Graduate Studies, Monarch Business School, Switzerland; an MSc in Banking and Finance from the University of Lausanne, Switzerland; an MBA from London South Bank University; and a BSc in Computer Science from the Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology. He is a Certified Treasury Analyst and has a number of publications to his credit.

Mr. Nelson is a KPMG-trained chartered accountant, with the ACCA qualification from the United Kingdom (2005) and the ICA qualification from Ghana (2007), and a Certificate in Structured Finance from the African Export-Import Bank. He has significant experience in energy and mining, and in financial and business analysis across insurance and investment banking.

He is Managing Director of Best Pensions Trust Limited and has held finance leadership in commodities, including refined petroleum and soft commodities, and group financial reporting. He has managed a balance sheet of over $230 million in assets in the financial sector.

At KPMG he worked on audits including Total Petroleum Ghana Limited and the Bank of Ghana, and on assignments in banking, the downstream petroleum sector, and Bibiani Gold Mine.

He holds board seats at Best Pensions Trust Ltd, Radcon Belma Ltd, Vobatex Express Ltd, and other organisations.

Dr. Sydney O. Vanderpuye is Director, Finance and Accounting, of the ECOWAS Bank for Investment and Development, a role he assumed in July 2017. He is a Fellow of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants and a practice member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants, Ghana. He holds a Doctor of Business Administration from IPAG Business School, France, an MBA in Finance from London South Bank University, and a BSc in Administration (Accounting) from the University of Ghana. He is an alumnus of the Advanced Strategic Management Program at IMD, Switzerland.

He has worked in UK public-sector accounting with the London Borough of Barking and Dagenham, then with Ernst & Young Ghana on audit and consultancy assignments. In 2006 he was a pioneer staff member of Intercontinental Bank Ghana Limited, later taken over by Access Bank. He joined Société Générale in 2012 as Head of Accounts and was subsequently appointed Head of Finance.

He has facilitated training for financial institutions in Ghana and has covered accounting and performance measurement in banking at the University of Ghana Business School.

Rev. Dr. Abel Yeboah-Ofori holds a PhD in Cyber Security from the University of East London. He is a Senior Lecturer in Cyber Security at the University of West London, School of Computing and Engineering. He has an MSc in Information Security and Digital Forensics from the University of East London and a BSc (Hons) in Computing and Business Information Systems. He holds a PgCert in Higher Education Practice, is a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy, a PRINCE2 practitioner, and a certified cyber security and digital forensics investigations practitioner.

He consults in cyber security and digital forensics, has published, and was invited to the Cyber Security Maturity Assessment programme with the Global Cyber Security Capacity Centre at the University of Oxford and the World Bank. He was also invited to an advisory and review workshop on national cyber security policy and strategy by the Ministry of Communications and the Council of Europe, as part of GLACY+ activities.

His fields include cyber security, machine learning, digital forensics, cyber threat intelligence, threat modelling, and cyber supply-chain risk.

Nathaniel Hegbor is a lawyer and a notary with over twenty years’ standing at the bar. He is a chartered banker and a chartered development finance analyst. He has over sixteen years of experience in the banking and financial services sector, in a range of roles.

His areas of expertise include corporate law, banking and finance, corporate governance, and compliance. He is a former adjunct lecturer in company law and commercial law at the University of Ghana Business School, and teaches corporate banking law and practice at the Chartered Institute of Bankers, Ghana.

He is Managing Partner of Zoe Lexfields, a private law firm in Accra, and serves Reality International & Partners as Legal Consultant and Company Secretary.
