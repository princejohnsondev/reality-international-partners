The firm

A boutique practice, built around the client’s objective.

Reality International & Partners advises private institutions and individuals in Ghana. The work is strategy and investment advice: who to back, how to fund it, and how to keep the business healthy once the capital is in.

Accra

Advice for people building in Ghana.

Private clients, growing companies, and regional firms expanding their operations here.

Who we sit with

Individuals looking for investment opportunities. Small and mid-sized companies looking for capital. Regional corporations expanding their operations in Ghana.

Where the work sits

Banking, insurance, fintech, and the development of small and mid-sized businesses — in Ghana, and with an eye on the wider continent.

How we begin

We study the client’s position before we propose anything. The recommendation is specific. A standard deck is not a strategy.

What we owe

Integrity in the advice, care in the execution, and a result the client can use. The relationship is the work, not a brochure around it.

Vision

A reference firm for serious financial advice in Ghana and across Africa.

The aim is a practice known for banking, insurance, fintech, and SME advisory — prompt, careful, and inventive where the situation calls for it.

Mission. Stand apart by the usefulness of the advice. Each client has a different constraint. The work should feel made for that constraint, and it should arrive while it can still change the outcome.

What we hold

Six commitments. They govern the advice and the way the firm behaves around it.

Innovation

Room for a better idea, and the discipline to adopt it when it improves the client’s position.

Responsibility

Ethical conduct and the courage to set conditions for high performance, individually and as a team.

Accountability

Integrity

Service

Partnership
