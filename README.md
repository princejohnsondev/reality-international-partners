## What changed, and why it is better

The old site was a broken mirror: duplicate pages, two menus, repeated copy, and another firm’s name still on the page. The new site is one clear brochure.

- **One identity.** The header, address, phones, and three official emails are the firm’s own. Visitors reach the right office.
- **Fewer pages.** Home, firm, services, leadership, and contact replace the repeated service and about pages. Old addresses still redirect, so nothing is lost.
- **A readable practice.** Nine services are short cards. Each one says what the firm actually does.
- **Leadership you can open.** A portrait opens the full resume: career, qualifications, and boards.
- **A contact page that answers.** The office, the three lines, and the form sit together. A completed note ends on a thank-you page.
- **A calmer design.** The logo, navy type, and a little green replace heavy green blocks. Photography and a short Accra section carry the firm, without invented awards or client names.
- **Works on a phone.** The full menu stays on a desktop. A phone gets a simple menu, and the pages reflow instead of overflowing.
