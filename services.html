Services

Advice that can be executed.

We are practical. The recommendation has to be viable, and it has to leave the client’s standards intact. Integrity is not a footnote to the transaction.

Capital Advisory Restructuring Risk Debt recovery Assurance Management AML Cyber

01

Capital raising

We raise capital for companies of different sizes and sectors in Ghana and in other African markets. The work runs from preparation and capital structure through investor discussions, due diligence, negotiation, and closing.

  • Equity
  • Convertible debt and quasi-equity
  • Debt

02

Financial advisory

Insight on the decision, and the discipline to carry a transaction through. The approach is pragmatic: a solution that can be sustained, delivered without compromising ethical standards.

03

Corporate restructuring and recovery

A company under strain needs a clear view before it chooses a path. We bring the specialised work together so the client can change the operating and financial position, not only describe it.

  • Restructuring, turnaround, and reorganisation planning
  • Operating and financial efficiency
  • Bankruptcy and insolvency management
  • Distressed sell-side and buy-side advice

04

Risk management

Risk advice that management can use. We help design policies and procedures aligned with regulatory standards and sound practice, simplify the operation where it has grown tangled, and protect the reputation of the firm.

  • Assessment, strategy, and optimisation
  • Frameworks, policies, and procedures
  • Implementation and management
  • Governance and monitoring

05

Debt recovery

Recovery for banks, savings and loans, microfinance institutions, insurers, and commercial, government, and retail creditors. We negotiate disputes with care and with pace, and we aim to recover outside court whenever that serves the client.

06

Revenue assurance

Underperformance is often a leak, not a missing sale. We look for the points where revenue and cash leave the business, and we work on closing them. Tax and corporate questions that sit beside that work are supported by the firm’s technical review.

07

Business management and monitoring

An outside view of how the business is actually running: performance, the commitments already made, and whether the plan still matches the facts. The point is a clearer decision, taken in time.

08

Anti-money laundering diligence

A risk-based approach to AML requirements: screening and enhanced due diligence shaped to the client, the jurisdiction, and the design of the programme. We work as an outsourced or overflow team, on enhanced reviews of higher-risk relationships, and on remediation or look-back exercises.

  • Background screening, sanctions and watch-list checks
  • Identification and documentation checks for investors and account holders
  • Litigation, public-record, and source enquiries

09

Cyber security

Security work grounded in the business, from the first assessment through to a managed arrangement. The aim is protection the organisation can keep, not a report that sits on a shelf.

  • Awareness that lasts beyond a single session
  • Information security assessment and IT audit
  • Penetration testing and phishing exercises
  • Business continuity
  • Security policy, and an outsourced CISO
